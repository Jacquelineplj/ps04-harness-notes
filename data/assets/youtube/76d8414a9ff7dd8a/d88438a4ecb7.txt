Welcome to LangChain Academy. Here, we offer courses and certification programs to help you grow as an agent engineer. Before you dive in, I'd love to give you a quick overview to help you find the resource that works best for you. Our goal at LangChain Academy is to teach you how to ship great agents and improve them continuously. We've learned that the best way to build agents is with a continuous agent development life cycle. Build, test,
deploy, and monitor. So, we've built our library of courses around those stages. To learn how to build your agent, we'll walk you through our ecosystem of agent frameworks: LangChain, LangGraph, and Deep Agents, as well as fundamental concepts you should know regardless of what you're building. To learn how to test, deploy, and monitor your agent, we'll walk you through different parts of LangSmith, our framework-agnostic platform for
agent engineering. Testing your agent requires evals, data sets, and experiments, so you can be confident enough to move to production. LangSmith provides these as first-class features within the platform. Deploying an agent surfaces unique challenge requiring unique infrastructure. You can maintain that infrastructure yourself, or you can deploy quickly with LangSmith deployments, sandboxes, and manage Deep Agents.
Monitoring your agent with LangSmith traces tells you how it really behaves in front of users. With evals, insights, and engine, you can scale your observability to meet production capacity. And finally, organizations need governance over this process. Security and cost monitoring need to be proactive, not reactive, using tools like LangSmith LLM Gateway. Within each of these topics, you'll find courses of varying length and complexity.
Quick start courses are the shortest and should be the jumping off point for anyone not sure where to begin. Once you've begun to master the basics of agent engineering, challenge yourself with one of our industry certifications. Our first, the LangChain certified agent engineer, requires a deep understanding of all four stages of the ADLC, proven by passing a rigorous proctored exam. I'm so excited that you've joined us
here at LangChain Academy to sharpen your agent engineering skills and hone your intelligence. I look forward to seeing what you build first.
