As AI teams scale their evaluation workflows, the cost of running those evaluations can add up. LLM-as-a-Judge evaluations often rely on powerful frontier models, even when smaller, less expensive models may be sufficient for the task.
The challenge is knowing where a lower-cost model can deliver reliable results without compromising evaluation quality.
Arize AX now supports Together AI as an AI provider for evaluations, making it easier to test open models, compare performance, and explore opportunities to reduce evaluation costs without configuring custom endpoints.
Together AI provides access to a broad selection of open models through an OpenAI-compatible API. With this integration, teams can use supported Together AI models for LLM-as-a-Judge evaluations, data labeling, and model comparisons in the Arize AX Playground.
Agent evaluations help teams measure and improve AI application quality. But running evaluations at scale introduces its own costs, particularly when every test requires an additional call to an expensive model.
Consider a team using a frontier model to evaluate thousands of customer support responses for correctness. Each evaluation adds to the overall cost of operating the AI system.
A smaller open model might be able to handle the same evaluation task at a lower cost. Before making the switch, however, the team needs to understand how closely its judgments align with the existing evaluator and whether it reliably identifies the same failures.
With Together AI configured as an AI provider in Arize AX, teams can test open models against their existing evaluation criteria and compare results. If a lower-cost model delivers sufficiently reliable judgments, it may be a better fit for running evaluations at scale.

Once connected, you can use supported Together AI models to run evaluations, generate labels, and compare model performance in the Playground.
